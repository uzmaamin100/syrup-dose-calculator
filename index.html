Children's Syrup Dose Calculator

Turns a weight-based prescription into the exact millilitres of syrup to give, with built-in checks that catch common dosing mistakes before they reach the child.

For study use only. Always confirm the dose with a pharmacist or doctor.

Why this calculator exists

⚖️

Children's doses depend on weight

Most syrups for children are prescribed in mg per kg, so every child needs their own calculation.

🧮

mg to mL is easy to get wrong

Mixing up "per dose" and "per day", or misreading the bottle strength, can mean giving too much or too little.

🛡️

Safety first

The app warns, caps doses above the maximum, and shows every step so a pharmacist can check it in seconds.
